P9 Sans
=======

P9 Sans is a derivative of Roboto (https://github.com/googlefonts/roboto),
which is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

Modifications made for this project:
  - Horizontal compression to 94% of original width
  - Dot-shaped glyph parts (. : i j ; !) replaced with squares
  - Family renamed to "P9 Sans"; PostScript names changed

The original Roboto license text is available at:
  http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

This derivative is distributed under the same Apache License 2.0.
